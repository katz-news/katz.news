Letters to the Editor

Agree, disagree, or want to add something? Write to letters@katz.news.

  • Keep it under 250 words, and respond to something we published in the last four weeks.
  • Include your name and your city or country. We publish letters under the name you give us.
  • We may edit for length and clarity, and we cannot publish every letter.
  • If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you write.
