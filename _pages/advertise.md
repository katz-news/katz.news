## Our rules

- Advertising and sponsorship never influence what we report.
- Every ad and sponsored piece is clearly labelled as such.
- We do not use tracking or advertising cookies, and we never sell reader data.
- We decline advertising that conflicts with our [Standards](/about/standards/) or targets young readers inappropriately.
