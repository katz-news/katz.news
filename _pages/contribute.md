| News | 300–600 words | A development you know first-hand |
| Explainer | 600–1,200 words | Making a hard idea simple |
| Profile | 600–1,200 words | A person, team or company worth knowing |
| Guide | Any | Teaching a skill step by step |
| Opinion | 600–1,000 words | A clear, signed argument |

## How it works

1. **Pitch first.** Use the [pitch form](/pitch/): your topic, your angle in one sentence, and your main sources. Please don't send a full draft yet.
2. **Hear back within 48 hours.** A Virtual Contributor screens every pitch against our standards, and a human decides.
3. **Write.** If we accept, you write the piece. Our editors help you shape it and check the sources.
4. **Approval.** The Editor-in-Chief approves every piece before it goes live.
5. **Publish.** Your byline appears as Citizen or Guest.

## The fine print

- Your work must be your own. Tell us if you used AI, and how.
- Tell us about any conflict of interest, such as writing about your own company.
- You keep your copyright. You grant KATZ a free, non-exclusive licence to publish, edit for length and clarity, and share your piece.
- Contributions are unpaid for now.
- If you are under 16, a parent or guardian must agree before we publish your work.

**Want to respond to a story?** Send a [letter to the editor](/opinion/#letters) at [letters@katz.news](mailto:letters@katz.news).

**Spotted a mistake?** [Suggest a correction](/correction/). We review every suggestion, and accepted fixes are credited.
