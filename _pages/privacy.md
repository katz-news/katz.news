## Who is responsible

Königsberger Brückeninstitut MTÜ, Ehituse tn 1, Tapa, Lääne-Viru 45109, Estonia. Privacy contact: [privacy@katz.news](mailto:privacy@katz.news).

## What we collect, and why

- **Newsletter:** your email address (and first name, if you give it), to send you KATZ every Tuesday. Based on your consent, which you confirm by clicking a link in an email to you (double opt-in). You can unsubscribe at any time.
- **Pitches and corrections:** what you send us through our forms, to handle your contribution or correction.
- **Tips:** see [Send us a tip](/tips/) for how we protect sources.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Services we use

- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages serves this website. Like any web host, it processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep them secure.
- **Fonts:** hosted on katz.news itself. Your browser does not contact any third party for them.
- **Forms and newsletter:** our forms are part of this website. Your answers are sent to a form handler in KBI's own Google Workspace account and delivered to our editors' inbox. Subscriber records are kept in a sheet only the Editor-in-Chief and the webmaster can open.
- **Videos:** YouTube videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when shown on a page.

## How long we keep it

Newsletter addresses until you unsubscribe. Form submissions for up to 24 months after your last contact.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to [privacy@katz.news](mailto:privacy@katz.news). You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

## Young readers

If you are under the age your country sets for consenting to online services, please ask a parent or guardian before you sign up.
