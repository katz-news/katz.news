Doctors are detectives. Microsoft's MAI-DxO is a whole detective agency. Powered by GPT-5, the system acts like a physician panel: one specialist proposes diagnoses, another orders tests and challenges them, a third counts the cost, and an orchestrator runs the debate. On 304 fiendishly complex cases from the New England Journal of Medicine, it diagnosed correctly 83 percent of the time, against 22 percent for doctors relying on their own knowledge. On typical emergency-room cases it scored 80 percent to the doctors' 47. The verdict is doctor-defyingly diagnostic: the AI was also cheaper, at about $1,300 a case against over $4,700.
