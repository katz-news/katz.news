Wonder wants a bigger bite. The delivery startup has filed for an IPO, after raising $650 million in July at a valuation of $9 billion. Founded in 2018 by Marc Lore of Jet.com and Diapers.com, Wonder runs locations where customers order from restaurant brands on one platform, handling cooking and delivery. It has swallowed Blue Apron, Grubhub, Tastemade and kitchen-robotics firm Spyce, and plans more than 100 Texas locations by the end of 2027. The verdict is wonderfully wobbly: a delivery app that became a food empire must now convince public markets it can chew what it has bitten off.
