Texas built the AI boom. Texas may now unbuild it. The state is the fastest-growing data center market in America, home to the 1,820-megawatt Stargate Frontier centre and the 6,400-megawatt Nexus Hubbard site. Then the midterms delivered a blue wave: James Talarico won the Senate seat, Gina Hinojosa won the governorship, and Democrats took the Texas House for the first time in 24 years, with data centers a central campaign issue. The verdict is texanly turbulent: a moratorium could stall the buildout, but local officials, construction unions and a patchwork of approvals may make governing far messier than campaigning.
