Hard work rarely speaks for itself. Brian Jenney learned that slowly: five years, two job switches and 12-hour days, without a promotion. He only got one after interviewing and quitting, when his employer counter-offered and HR asked, 'Why didn’t you just ask us?' His fix is a brag document: a list of what you accomplished, when, and why it mattered, updated every two weeks. Write entries so your manager’s manager understands them. Then ask what it would take to reach the next level. The verdict is plainly promotional: waiting to be noticed costs years; asking costs an awkward conversation.
