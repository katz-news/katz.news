Your watch may know your blood pressure. Hypertension is a silent killer, and more than half of affected adults do not know they have it. Google researchers have trained an AI, AI-PPG-ACC-HTN, to spot it from a consumer smartwatch, reading pulse signals and movement, with no cuff and no calibration. In a prospective study of 196 participants, validated against gold-standard 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure monitoring over seven days, it caught 65.8% of cases, versus 55.3% for office blood pressure screening. The verdict is wristy wisdom: the best blood pressure test may be the one you never notice taking.
