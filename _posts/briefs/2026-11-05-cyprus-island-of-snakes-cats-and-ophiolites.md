Snake skin, ruled by cats. Cyprus began forming 90 million years ago, when molten rock from the seafloor built new oceanic crust. Tectonic forces wrenched it upward into an island of ophiolite, a mottled green rock said to look like snake skin. Antiquity knew Cyprus as the land of snakes, home to blunt-nosed vipers. Legend says Saint Helena, mother of Constantine the Great, shipped in cats from Egypt to control them during a drought. Today one million cats stalk the island, one for every resident. The verdict is cattily crusty: an ancient ocean floor, now ruled by cats.
