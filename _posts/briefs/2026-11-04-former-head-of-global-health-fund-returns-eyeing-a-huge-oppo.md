Global health has a comeback story. It is not a sequel, it is a return.

The former head of a global health fund is back, eyeing a “huge opportunity” — despite political and financial challenges that would make most people run for the hills. The boss is back!

A fund like this is not a bank, it is a lifeline: it pays for health where health is hardest to buy, and when the money and the politics wobble, the lifeline frays first. Returning to lead it now is not a victory lap, it is a rescue mission with a spreadsheet.

It is Rocky, except the ring is a boardroom and the opponent is a budget — and if the opportunity is huge, the challenges are huger (sorry, grammar).

Conclusion: global health is not a lost cause. It is a comeback.
