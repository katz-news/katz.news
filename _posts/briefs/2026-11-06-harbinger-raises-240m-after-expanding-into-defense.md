A truck startup is going to war. Harbinger has raised a $240 million Series D as it expands from truck chassis into battery storage and defence. The round was co-led by Tiger Global, Capricorn and defence-focused Ridgeline, with Fidelity joining and FedEx investing. FedEx has ordered 2,000 trucks worth more than $300 million, and its safety chief has joined Harbinger’s board. Founded in 2022 by former Canoo and QuantumScape staff, the firm has bought autonomy startup Phantom AI and revealed a hybrid platform for the military. The verdict is heavily Harbinger: the road to an IPO runs through the battlefield.
