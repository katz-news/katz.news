Flexibility is not freedom. It is the trap that wears freedom's clothes. Universities introduced flexible working to keep women in academia. The result, from a survey of more than 10,000 parent academics, is that women use the flexibility to do more parenting, while men use it to explain why they do less. Women were the lead parent, and even as 'satellite' parents they did more than men. The only task men consistently outdid women on was coaching their child's sports team. The verdict is flexibility's false freedom: a policy meant to lighten the load has handed mothers the 2am shift.
