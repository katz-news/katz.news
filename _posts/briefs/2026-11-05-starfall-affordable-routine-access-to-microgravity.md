Gravity is a manufacturing defect. SpaceX's Starfall offers affordable, routine access to microgravity in low-Earth orbit, launching on Starship for a reliable, scalable cadence. Drop towers and parabolic flights last seconds; Starfall provides weeks of continuous weightlessness, with 1,000 kilograms of payload, a kilowatt of power and continuous Starlink connectivity. Without gravity, biology accelerates and reorganises: superior implants, layered biomaterials, stem-cell organoids and replacement tissue. Proteins form smaller, more uniform crystals. The verdict is weightlessly wonderful: the factory of the future may have no floor at all.
