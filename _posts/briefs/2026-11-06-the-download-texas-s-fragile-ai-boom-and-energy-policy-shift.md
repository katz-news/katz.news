Texas built the wall the data centre boom just hit. Texas is America’s fastest-growing data centre market, but a blue wave has swept it: James Talarico won the US Senate seat, Gina Hinojosa won the governorship, and Democrats take the Texas House for the first time in 24 years. Data centres were a campaign issue. Voters opposed them in 40 of 41 midterm ballot measures, and six cities banned them. The verdict is bitterly bipartisan: the AI boom is not an engineering problem, but rather a political one. Energy policy turns on state grids, not Washington.
