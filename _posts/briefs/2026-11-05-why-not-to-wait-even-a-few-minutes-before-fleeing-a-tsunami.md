Minutes are the difference between a story and a statistic. In Viña del Mar, Chile, researchers tracked 119 GPS routes from tsunami evacuation drills between 2022 and 2026 and matched them against simulated flooding from a subduction earthquake. Leave immediately and 100 of the 119 routes reach safety. Wait eight minutes for the warning and only 49 make it. Wait twelve, until the water is already arriving, and just 3 survive. The routes split across 13 corridors, and neighbouring paths fared wildly differently. The verdict is delay's deadly arithmetic: what matters is not where you run, but when you start.
