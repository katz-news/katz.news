Nostalgia, now walking across your browser. MarkBlah has resurrected the lemmings of the 1991 Psygnosis classic as a CSS animation playground, using sprites pulled from the original game. Add walkers, drag one to carry it, flick it to throw it, and watch them march over stairs, floating platforms, pits and walls built from the page itself. Text is a floor they can land on but walk through; borders are solid. Resize the window and the walkers ride the surface, so a fast resize launches them. The verdict is playfully pixelated: thirty-five years on, the little green-haired marchers still refuse to stop.
