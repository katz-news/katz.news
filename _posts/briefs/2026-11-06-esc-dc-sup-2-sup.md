A hacker walks into a data centre. Taik Hobson’s story sends an A-hat hacker through Icelandic snow to Skýborg, a decommissioned cloud centre that is alive. Her C-Skin disguise turns her into an anthropoid with an orb in her forehead. Inside, the servers are drones. The security is thought-scanners. Her job is simple: erase data with a SEDkey, a crowbar that secure-erases and degausses. The ransom demand is €12.49, or the value of pi. The verdict is deliciously data-centred: the safest cloud is a dead one, and the best hacker is the one who walks through the front door.
