A mystery gadget, with no screen and no app. Nothing CEO Carl Pei has announced that the company's first AI-native product ships in December, calling it a productivity tool built with the personality and care that define a Nothing product. It has no new screen, and it could not have been an app. Pei has teased an AI-first device for some time, and Nothing also plans AI-linked smart glasses and earbuds with AI integrations. The verdict is mysteriously magnetic: the whole industry is hunting for its iPhone moment, and Pei insists he reads this moment differently.
