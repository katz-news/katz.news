The midterms were a blue wave. The data centres felt it first.

Democrats won historic victories in this week's US midterm elections, and the tech industry is bracing for the consequences. Voters decided 41 data-centre ballot measures on Tuesday. In all but one, the majority voted for more restrictions on data-centre development.

Even where data centres were not on the ballot, they dominated the debate. Newly elected Democratic governors and members of Congress are likely to back further regulation, and Big Tech can expect more government scrutiny. OpenAI is already trying to rebrand the data centre.

The Equity podcast also covers the week's other tech news: Google's first satellite data centre, layoffs at Canva and Squarespace, Marvell's $115 billion purchase of Lumentum, and CrowdStrike's acquisition of two AI security startups.

Conclusion: the election is not a policy, but rather a power cut — and the AI industry just felt the switch flip.
