Your hard hat is reading your mind. Almost. In China, 2,000 NeuroHua helmets hide EEG sensors that track construction workers’ brain waves, movement and blood oxygen, claiming over 95% accuracy in spotting fatigue. The company says it has prevented multiple major accidents. The catch is the raw brain data is collected and processed. Another firm pipes truck drivers’ brain waves, names and locations to traffic police. Elsewhere, Australia’s SmartCap caps send fatigue scores every three seconds. Workers once gamed them by staring at dashboards. The verdict is brutally brainy: safety helmets save lives, but the price is your private thoughts.
