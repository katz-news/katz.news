Hubble is falling, and NASA wants a rescue plan. The telescope, launched in 1990 and fixed in 1993 after its mirror flaw, faces re-entry around 2032 as atmospheric drag pulls it down. Its orbit boost came from Columbia. 23,000 papers came from its images, and it sees ultraviolet light its successors cannot. NASA has asked the private sector for ideas, with submissions due by 30 November, to raise Hubble to 600 kilometres or guide it into the ocean. The verdict is heavenly Hubble: the telescope that taught us the universe is asking us to save it.
