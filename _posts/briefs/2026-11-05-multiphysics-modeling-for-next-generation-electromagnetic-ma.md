One simulation, every physics at once. This webinar shows how COMSOL Multiphysics couples electromagnetic fields with thermal, chemical, mechanical and transport effects to model next-generation manufacturing. First up is eddy-current thermometry, which reconstructs three-dimensional temperature fields inside metal plates from the way heat changes electrical conductivity. Then comes microwave-enabled volumetric additive manufacturing, where a high-power beamforming array cures opaque, particle-laden resins into ceramic components. The verdict is multiply marvellous: fewer experiments, better predictions, and a faster route from simulation to scale-up. Presented by Dr Saptarshi Mukherjee of Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.
