The oldest art hid in plain sight. Hundreds of rock-art engravings, around 26,000 years old, have been found in the Cova de la Font Major in Catalonia, a region thought devoid of palaeolithic art. Archaeologist Josep Maria Vergès Bosch spotted them among modern graffiti in 2019. He and Míriam Salas-Altès studied 280 motifs, including 55 animal depictions: a horse, a bear and an aurochs, ancestor of domestic cattle. The engravings were easy to miss. Shine a light on them and they vanish; play with the light and they appear. The verdict is plainly prehistoric: old art hides in busy places.
