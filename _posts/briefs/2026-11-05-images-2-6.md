Four billion images a week, and now they move. OpenAI's Images 2.6 generates pictures 50 percent faster and finally adds motion: GIFs that animate a brand mascot, a character or a product shot into a looping reaction. The API offers two flavours, Flare for speed (as little as six seconds at low quality) and Sunburst for detail and consistency across edits, plus SVG exports, image markup and a new complexity control. People already create more than half a billion images a day. The verdict is giddily gif-able: still images were so last week; the internet's native language now comes with a prompt.
