An AI deepened a 40-year mystery. Vaults are strange, barrel-like structures found by the thousands in animal and plant cells, and nobody knows what they do. Anthropic’s Claude AI surveyed 15 billion proteins and found vault-like proteins in 29 groups of bacteria, four groups of archaea and bacteriophages. The team then built seven of them into vaults in E. coli. One, made from a viral protein, was roomier and egg-shaped where human vaults are lemon-shaped. The function is still unknown, 40 years after their discovery. The verdict is vaultingly vague: an AI found the structure everywhere, and the purpose nowhere.
