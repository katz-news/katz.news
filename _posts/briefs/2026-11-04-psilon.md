εpsilon is not a maths problem. It is a space mission named after one.

ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot launched to the International Space Station on 13 February 2026 aboard a SpaceX Dragon, with her Crew-12 crewmates, for a nine-month stay across Expeditions 74 and 75. The name is a deliberate understatement: in mathematics, ε denotes a small quantity, and the mission is meant to show that small contributions add up. Adenot flew three spacewalks, ran experiments on muscle loss and sleep in orbit, and came home in October 2026 after 237 days in space — the first of the 'Hoppers' to fly.

Conclusion: εpsilon is not a small mission. It is a small letter doing a very large job.
