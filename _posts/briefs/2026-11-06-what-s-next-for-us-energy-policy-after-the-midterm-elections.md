The blue wave hit Washington. Democrats took both the House and the Senate, yet Donald Trump is still president, without a veto-proof majority. Do not expect huge reversals right away. The action is elsewhere: the Senate votes on 9 November on the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act, a permitting overhaul. Democrats will scrutinise Trump’s agencies, including EPA commissioner Lee Zeldin. Locally, Pennsylvania backed renewables and Georgia’s utility regulator flipped after 30 years. Voters restricted data centres in nearly all 41 ballot questions. The verdict is divided but decisive: Washington is gridlocked, and the grid is where the change is.
