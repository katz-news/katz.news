America's oldest natural science museum has closed. It survived 214 years, two centuries and several wars, but not a balance sheet.

Drexel University, which acquired the Academy of Natural Sciences in 2011, announced on 1 September 2026 that it would close by the end of the month, citing financial difficulties and declining attendance; the last visitors left on 27 September. The William Penn Foundation offered to cover operating subsidies for at least another year, but Drexel says it needs a long-term solution, and Philadelphia City Council spent nearly four hours on the question on 26 October.

For the author (Lili Mei Granda Argianas, a physics PhD student), the Academy was where a first-generation college student first saw herself as a scientist — the same way a childhood visit to the blue whale hall had done years before.

Conclusion: a museum is not a building full of old bones, but rather a doorway, and this one just shut.
