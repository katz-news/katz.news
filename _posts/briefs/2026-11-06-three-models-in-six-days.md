Three labs shipped in six days. The race is not slowing down, but rather speeding up.

OpenAI opened the week on 23 April with GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.5 Pro. DeepSeek answered the next day with V4-Pro and V4-Flash, both carrying a million-token context window.

Mistral closed the week on 28 April with Medium 3.5. It is a dense 128-billion-parameter model with a 256,000-token window, released as open weights under a Modified MIT licence.

The contrast is the story. One lab sells access. Another gives away the weights. A third offers a million tokens and lets you read the whole library at once.

GPT-5.5 is the closed flagship. DeepSeek V4 is the open challenger, in two sizes: Pro at 1.6 trillion parameters, Flash at 284 billion. Mistral sits between them, frontier-class and downloadable.

Six days, three philosophies, one frantic finish. The models are converging in capability. The business models are diverging fast.
