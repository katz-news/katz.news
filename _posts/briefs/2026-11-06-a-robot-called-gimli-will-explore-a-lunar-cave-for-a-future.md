NASA’s safest moon base may be underground. The Moon is a murderous landlord. Its dust eats machinery. Its days hit 120°C. Its craters plunge towards absolute zero. Its pebble-sized asteroids arrive like hypersonic bullets. GIMLI is not a dwarf with an axe, but rather a robot with radar. The Geophysical Instruments for Marius Lunar pit Investigation will land beside the Marius Hills pit and map the suspected lava tube beneath it. Lava tubes offer shade, warmth and shielding from radiation. The verdict is deliciously dwarfish: the best lunar real estate is a dark, dusty, delightful dungeon.
