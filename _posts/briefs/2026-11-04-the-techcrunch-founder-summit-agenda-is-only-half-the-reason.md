The agenda is only half the point. The other half is the room.

TechCrunch Founder Summit 2026 took over SoWa Power Station in Boston on 4 November for one day of founder bootcamp: a Build Stage on distribution, fundraising and pitching (Hunter Worland of NEA, Kristen Craft of Fidelity Private Shares, Dave Balter of True Ventures), an Impact Stage on what happens after something starts working (Steve Fredette of Toast, Rudina Seseri of Glasswing Ventures), small roundtables on everything from AI hallucinations to enterprise selling, and a live pitch contest, 'So You Think You Can Pitch?', judged by investors including Balter and Simran Suri of Maveron.

Between sessions, Braindate networking matched founders one-to-one, and the day closed with a reception.

Conclusion: a founder summit is not a conference, but rather a hallway with a stage attached.
