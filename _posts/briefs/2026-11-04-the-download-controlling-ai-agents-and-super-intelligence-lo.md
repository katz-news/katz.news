Today's Download is a double bill: how to control AI agents, and how AI got a loyalty test.

First, reverse game theory — instead of reading an AI's mind, design the rules so it behaves. Researchers think it could align powerful models with our instructions; the catch is that 'aligning' is the hard part.

Second, a month after President Trump signed an executive order rebranding AI as 'Super Intelligence', the new term is creeping across government websites — and becoming a political loyalty test: use the phrase, signal allegiance.

Plus: today's midterms test AI's sway over voters, hackers turned supermarket fridges into a food-security risk, and Nvidia told data-centre clients to limit business with China.

Conclusion: the newsletter is not about AI, but rather about who gets to tell it what to do.
