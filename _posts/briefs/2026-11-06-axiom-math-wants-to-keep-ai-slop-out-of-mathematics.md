Mathematics has a slop problem. AI is cracking new problems weekly, yet even computer checks can slip. Axiom Math is not slowing maths down, but rather standing it on firmer ground. The $1.6 billion startup has formally verified the Stacks Project, one of the largest bodies of mathematics, translating roughly 7,654 pages into about 16 million lines of computer-checked Lean code. Its fleet of AI agents worked in parallel, finding their own tasks. Verified results become building blocks for future proofs, software and science. The verdict is marvellously mathematical: check the foundations first, and the slop has nowhere to stand.
