Death, but make it a subscription event. Some far-thinking technologists are working on ways to keep your brain going, even beyond death, in pursuit of an elusive continuity of the self. In this subscriber-only Roundtable, MIT Technology Review's Mary Beth Griggs and Antonio Regalado explore the possible paths: gradual brain replacement, little by little, and uploading our memories to the cloud. The conversation goes live on Tuesday 17 November. The verdict is immortally ironic: the quest to live forever begins with a conversation you must pay to join.
