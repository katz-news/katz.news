Your next computer is a ring. It has a joystick — and a job: to boss your bots.

Aina, a hardware startup that raised $5.5 million in July, has released its 'AI ring': a $399 smart ring with a screen, a joystick, a microphone and a speaker. Talk to your AI agents, take notes, set reminders, dictate on your Mac, take calls — and, when the Apple TV remote vanishes, control the television too.

The logic is the pitch. Phones were built for browsing, says founder Apoorv Shankar; agents need a better interface. The ring is that interface, worn on a finger, aimed squarely at people already surrounded by agents.

It is not a fitness tracker, but rather a remote control for your AI life. Early-bird preorders open Thursday; shipping starts in January 2027, when the price rises to $499.
