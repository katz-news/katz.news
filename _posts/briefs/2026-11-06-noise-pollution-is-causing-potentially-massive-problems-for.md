Everywhere we go, we bring the noise. A massive analysis in Science, covering 214 studies of 177 species, found human noise alters at least one trait in 80% of species. It changes gene expression, immune function and respiration, and rewires foraging, movement and parental care. Bats hunting moths by sound must work 2.5 times harder for a quarter less prey, while shore crabs are driven from shelters that amplify ship noise. Abundance fell roughly 40% in noisy areas, and mortality rose almost 140%. The verdict is deafeningly damning: the world is getting louder, and wildlife is paying for our racket.
