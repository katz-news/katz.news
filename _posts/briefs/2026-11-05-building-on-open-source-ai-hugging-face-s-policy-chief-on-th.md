Free models, expensive questions. Open source models are going mainstream as startups, enterprises and governments chase lower costs and more control. But if the models are free, who makes the money? On TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, Hugging Face policy chief Irene Solaiman explains what startups need to know before building on them. The trade-offs: when the biggest model is not the right one, whether builders can capture the value they create, and whether America needs a player like Nvidia to build a frontier model before Chinese models become the default. The verdict is openly obvious: models are not free of consequences.
