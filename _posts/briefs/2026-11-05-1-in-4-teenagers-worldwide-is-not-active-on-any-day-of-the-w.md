Fifteen years of standing still. One in four adolescents worldwide reported not a day of at least 60 minutes of physical activity in the previous week. That is 240 million young people aged 11 to 17. On average they were active 2.7 days a week. Girls are falling furthest behind: 28 percent reported no active days, against 22 percent of boys. The answer is not to tell teenagers to try harder, but to build schools, streets and communities where moving is safe, easy and normal. The verdict is plainly painful: the kids are not lazy, but rather the world is.
