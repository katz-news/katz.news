Free models, expensive questions. Open source AI is booming as enterprises, governments and startups embrace the promise of lower costs, more control and more flexibility. But when increasingly capable models are freely available, how does anyone capture value? On TechCrunch's Equity podcast, Rebecca Bellan puts the question to Irene Solaiman, Hugging Face's chief policy officer, covering what startups must know before building on open models, from tools and infrastructure to the trade-offs shaping open AI's future. The verdict is openly opportunistic: with Chinese open models gaining ground, can American players keep up?
