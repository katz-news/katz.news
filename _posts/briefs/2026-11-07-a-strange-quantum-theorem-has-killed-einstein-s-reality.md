Einstein lost. Reality lost with him — or at least, the reality he believed in.

Einstein wanted the world to have objective facts: a ball is red or green whether or not anyone looks. Quantum mechanics said otherwise, and for a century physicists tried to rescue his view. Bell's theorem closed one escape route. The PBR theorem, from Pusey, Barrett and Rudolph, closed the last: the wave function cannot be mere information about reality. It must be real itself.

A 2012 experiment confirmed it. A Cambridge test last autumn confirmed it again. What survives are two extremes: a surreal reality, or no description of reality at all.

Conclusion: the quantum state is not a weather forecast, but rather the weather — a wild, woolly world where Einstein's commonsense reality simply cannot survive.
