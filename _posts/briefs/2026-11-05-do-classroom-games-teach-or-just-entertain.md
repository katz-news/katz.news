A lively classroom is not the same as a learning classroom. Kahoot! says more than 10 million teachers use its platform, and quiz games now punctuate the school week. But do they teach, or just entertain? Liz Crawford of Kahoot! says engagement is meaningless unless it drives outcomes, pointing to more than 300 peer-reviewed studies and students scoring about a grade better with consistent use. Matthew Farber, of the University of Northern Colorado, warns that a quiz game is almost always a review with a timer, and grading one is a slippery slope. His advice is playfully pointed: put play ahead of points.
