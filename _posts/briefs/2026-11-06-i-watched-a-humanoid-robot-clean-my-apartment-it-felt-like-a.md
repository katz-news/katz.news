Tau Robotics sends humanoid robots to clean San Francisco homes, for $60 a week with two robots or $100 for a one-off visit. The robots are piloted remotely by humans watching through cameras in their heads and wrists. They climb stairs as if drunk, and a carpeted staircase defeated one entirely. Yet the same robot cleaned a kitchen, saved its owner the dreaded toilet, and reattached its cleaning wand three times after the head fell off. The verdict is tidily Tau: the future of housework is charming, clumsy and coming for your chores.
