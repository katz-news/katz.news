Oranges are not just breakfast. They are a mood.

Higher vitamin C intake during pregnancy is linked to fewer negative emotions, fussiness and distress in infants at 6 months — that is the finding, from r/science, and it is the kind of result that makes every pregnant person reach for the fruit bowl.

The link is a link, not a prescription: “linked to” is the careful language of a study, not a promise. Vitamin C is not a happy pill, it is a vitamin — and a baby's mood at six months is not a report card, it is a snapshot. And the intervention is fruit!

Still, the analogy holds like a good recipe: pregnancy is not a waiting room, it is a kitchen, and what goes into the kitchen shows up on the plate six months later (sorry, oranges). It is Popeye, except the spinach is citrus and the muscles are manners.

Conclusion: vitamin C is not a supplement. It is a head start.
