The best investment of the pandemic just doubled. Covid-19 vaccines were thought to have returned up to $38 trillion in their first year, on $79.4 billion spent developing them. A peer-reviewed reanalysis now puts the benefits at $6.43 trillion to $64.3 trillion. Every dollar spent returned between $59.70 and $809. The vaccines prevented an estimated 14 million to 20 million deaths in that first year. The catch is cruelly consistent: returns were highest in richer countries, because poorer ones got the vaccines later. The verdict is plainly profitable: protecting public health is protecting the economy.
