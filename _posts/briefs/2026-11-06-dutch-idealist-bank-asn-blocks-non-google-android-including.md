Europe’s answer to Visa has a Google problem. ASN Bank’s app, version 1.36.3, now refuses iDEAL | Wero payments on phones running GrapheneOS and other non-Google Android, saying the device fails its security checks. GrapheneOS passes Google’s basic integrity check, but not the profile check, and ASN follows iDEAL | Wero’s rules for operating systems. The bank says it is investigating support for alternative systems, with no promises. The irony is exquisite: Wero exists to free Europe from American payment giants, yet it leans on Google. The verdict is bitterly bankable: the idealist bank’s idealism stops where Google’s attestation starts.
